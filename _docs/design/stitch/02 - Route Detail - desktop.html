route Route Overview & Technical Waypoint Sequence

5 WAYPOINTS REGISTERED

The Mittenwalder Höhenweg is one of the premier ridge ferratas in the Northern Limestone Alps. Beginning either by cable car ascent or via the strenuous foot climb up the Dammkar, the technical route spans along the spine of the Karwendel peaks. Steel cables secure sheer rock faces, ladders span steep clefts, and airy bridges cross deep chasms. Complete surefootedness and zero vertigo are mandatory.

01

Karwendelbahn Bergstation → Westliche Karwendelspitze

ALT 2,244m → 2,385m · 35 MIN

Gentle scree traverse from the mountain station tunnel leading to the start of the fixed cable. Short scramble over stepped limestone (Difficulty A/B) leading directly to the summit cross.

02

Westl. Karwendelspitze → Nördliche Linderspitze

CRUX SECTION C · 1H 20M

The most demanding section of the via ferrata. Steel wire ropes along knife-edge ridges, stepped notches, and iron rungs on near-vertical walls. High exposure with 1,000-meter drops into the Karwendel valley.

03

Linderspitzen Notunterkunft (Emergency Refuge Box)

ALT 2,240m · CRITICAL SAFETY ESCAPE

Small fiberglass bivouac shelter for storm defense. A signposted escape descent branches down west to the Dammkarhütte (steep loose scree, helmet required).

04

Südliche Linderspitze → Brunnsteinanger

GRADE B/C · CABLE EXIT · 1H 15M

Traverse along jagged towers, descending through grassy crests and rocky steps. Fixed cables terminate at the Brunnsteinanger col (2,080m).

05

Brunnsteinhütte → Mittenwald Valley Return

DESCENT -1,160M · 1H 30M

Strenuous knee-taxing switchback trail down through pine dwarf scrub to the Brunnsteinhütte (serviced, freshwater tap). Crosses the famous suspension bridge (Hängebrücke) before returning to Mittenwald.
